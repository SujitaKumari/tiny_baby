    House of Baby Aura

    House
    of Baby Aura

    MANIFESTO Baby Aura is a modern parenthood house rooted in organic cotton, place and people. We honour our home’s textile traditions by carrying them forward through contemporary design, local craft, and clothing made to hold story.

    Our Roots

    Our story begins in the flax fields of our home, shaped by generations who lived and worked within the organic cotton industry.

    For us, Baby Aura is about belonging to something bigger than ourselves. Honouring our textile parenthood, feeling connected to those who came before us, and caring for the legacy we leave behind. We believe in making slowly, reimagining old techniques, and allowing the present to harness the power of the past.

    Our Founders

    Read their story

    Amy and Joel founded Baby Aura in 2020, on a shared belief that clothing should carry meaning, integrity and care.

    The house was sparked by a single photograph: a newspaper cutting of Amy's grandmother, Winnie, spinning organic cotton as a teenager. A quiet reminder of the lives and values that came before, and the thread it would become our work to carry forward.

    Read their story

    The Craft

    Discover our craft

    Craftsmanship sits at the heart of everything we make. We work with organic cotton and Nature tweed, using traditional techniques such as Irish crochet and beetled organic cotton: made slowly, locally, by the hands of skilled artisans.

    We design for longevity, not for the season: pieces meant to soften with wear, gather stories, and be handed on.

    Discover our craft

    Beetled Organic Cotton

    Among the crafts we carry, one is rarer than all the rest.

    Beetled organic cotton is finished the old way — the woven cloth dampened and beaten with wooden blocks for hours, until its surface compresses into a smooth, luminous sheen no modern process can imitate.

    Today it survives in just one place : the last weaving mill in the world, here in our home. Every beetled piece we make carries that finish: and helps keep a living craft from disappearing.

    Explore beetled organic cotton

    Sustainability

    Sustainability at Baby Aura is holistic and human. It encompasses how we design, how we make, how we treat people, and how we give back. From local production and considered materials to supporting charitable partners, we believe responsibility should sit at the heart of everything we do.

    Our Place

    Visit Us

    Belfast is the place that holds Baby Aura. It shapes our rhythm, our way of working, and our closeness to the makers and materials around us. By keeping production close to home, we build lasting relationships, support local skill, and stay rooted in the place our story began.
